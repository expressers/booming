## Platform

web

## Stack

Bun + React + Vite + Tailwind, with GSAP and Three.js (requested by Eládio). Lenis for smooth scroll and Phosphor icons chosen by Claude (delegated).

## Users

- Primary: facility managers, building owners and safety-conscious companies in Luanda with tall glass or panel facades. Many stopped cleaning their buildings because rope access and cranes put people at risk (Carlos, kickoff).
- Later: solar farm operators, owners of private jets (stated as future segments).

## Product Purpose

Booming Soluções Aéreas is a new segment of Booming (audiovisual agency since 2016, founder Carlos César). It cleans building facades with drones so no person has to hang from a rope. Success for the site: visitors understand the service in seconds and request a quote.

## Positioning

Aerial cleaning that removes the human risk of work at height. Carlos holds multiple drone certifications and is importing purpose-built cleaning drones (not adapted camera drones) from China in November 2026.

## Operating Context

- Tethered cleaning drone fed by a hose from a ground station; inspection and photographic report for the client.
- Go-to-market: free demonstrations for prospects, filmed by a second drone, to create the first real content.
- Separate from the Booming audiovisual site; the two brands must not be confused.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Now: facade and window cleaning.
- Planned: solar panels and photovoltaic fields, aerial mapping, structural inspection reports (cracks), private aviation cleaning.
- Site features agreed: quote form per service, contact routed to WhatsApp or social networks, client base section.
- Open: WhatsApp number, email, social handles, domain, per-service quote requirements (owed by Carlos).

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Booming, descriptor "Soluções aéreas". Slogan in the manual: "Beyond Reach".
- Positioning words: modernity and technology; high-end/luxury ambition. Tone axis from the manual: moderno, audaz, fluido, simples.
- No logo or icon that literally depicts a drone (Carlos, WhatsApp 06/10).
- Approved identity by Gláucio Salla: navy #192730, lime #91d336, greys #e6e6e6 / #1a1a1a, secondaries #2d3d4b, #3c3c3b, #adb65d; Lufga (titles) and Ubuntu (text). Note: in the kickoff Carlos asked to avoid green; the delivered identity uses lime. Following the identity until told otherwise.
- Language: Portuguese.
